# Course Roadmap

**Educentriks | Applied Data Science & AI/ML**

This roadmap reflects the approved 23-day, 32-hour curriculum blueprint.

## Module 1 — Foundations

| Day | Topic | Duration |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Data Science Lifecycle, Problem Formulation and Statistical Foundations | 1.5 hours |
| 2 | Experimental Design and A/B Testing | 1 hour |
| 3 | ML Pipeline, Data Preparation and Bias–Variance | 1.5 hours |

## Module 2 — Traditional Machine Learning

| Day | Topic | Duration |
|---|---|---:|
| 4 | Linear Regression and Regression Metrics | 1.5 hours |
| 5 | Logistic Regression, Confusion Matrix and Basic Classification Metrics | 1.5 hours |
| 6 | KNN and Naive Bayes | 1 hour |
| 7 | Decision Trees | 1.5 hours |
| 8 | Ensemble Methods and SVM | 1.5 hours |
| 9 | Unsupervised Learning and PCA | 1.5 hours |
| 10 | Advanced Model Validation, Class Imbalance and Feature Engineering | 1.5 hours |
| 11 | End-to-End ML Case Study and Forecasting Fundamentals | 1.5 hours |

## Module 3 — Deep Learning

| Day | Topic | Duration |
|---|---|---:|
| 12 | Neural Network Fundamentals | 1 hour |
| 13 | Building an ANN with PyTorch | 1.5 hours |
| 14 | Deep Learning Regularisation | 1.5 hours |
| 15 | CNN and Transfer Learning | 1.5 hours |
| 16 | RNN, LSTM, Attention and Transformers | 1.5 hours |
| 17 | Practical Deep Learning Case Study and Interview Preparation | 1 hour |

## Module 4 — NLP and Generative AI

| Day | Topic | Duration |
|---|---|---:|
| 18 | NLP Foundations and Vectorisation | 1.5 hours |
| 19 | Text Classification and Sentiment Analysis | 1 hour |
| 20 | Transformers, BERT and Hugging Face | 1.5 hours |
| 21 | Generative AI, LLMs and Lean RAG | 1.5 hours |

## Module 5 — Capstone and MLOps

| Day | Topic | Duration |
|---|---|---:|
| 22 | Capstone Integration, Streamlit Deployment and MLOps Concepts | 1.5 hours |
| 23 | Capstone Presentation, Technical Discussion and Mock Interview | 1.5 hours |

**Duration check:** 18 × 1.5 hours + 5 × 1 hour = 32 hours.

The programme assumes prior exposure to Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI, Tableau and basic EDA. These are prerequisites rather than subjects to reteach from scratch.
