# Day 1: Data Science Foundations

**Module 1: Data Science Foundations and Statistical Thinking**  
**Programme:** Educentriks | Applied Data Science & AI/ML

## 1. Day Overview

Welcome to Day 1 of the Applied Data Science & AI/ML programme.

Before building machine learning models, it is important to understand how data science solves business problems. In this session, we will explore the Data Science lifecycle, learn how to translate business questions into analytical problems and revisit essential statistical concepts used to understand data.

The emphasis is on developing the ability to think like a Data Scientist rather than simply learning algorithms or writing code.

## 2. Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, you should be able to:

1. Explain the difference between Data Science, Data Analytics and traditional reporting.
2. Describe the major stages of the Data Science lifecycle.
3. Translate a business question into an analytical or machine learning problem.
4. Understand population, sample, variables and common data types.
5. Interpret descriptive statistics, distributions and correlation.

## 3. Topics Covered

| Area | Topics |
|---|---|
| Data Science Fundamentals | Data Science vs Data Analytics vs Reporting |
| Problem Formulation | Business questions, analytical problems and ML problems |
| Data Science Lifecycle | Problem definition, data acquisition, preparation, exploration, modelling, evaluation, deployment and monitoring |
| Statistical Foundations | Population, sample, variables and data types |
| Descriptive Statistics | Mean, median, mode, variance and standard deviation |
| Statistical Thinking | Distributions and correlation vs causation |

## 4. Prerequisites

Students are expected to have:

- Basic Python programming knowledge.
- Familiarity with Excel and basic data analysis.
- Basic understanding of tables, rows, columns and datasets.
- A working Python environment configured according to the course setup guide.

No previous machine learning experience is required.

## 5. Classroom Practical

You will work with a small prepared business dataset and explore how a business problem can be translated into a Data Science problem.

The practical session will involve:

- Understanding a business scenario.
- Identifying the business question.
- Determining the data required to investigate the problem.
- Inspecting a small dataset.
- Calculating and interpreting descriptive statistics.
- Discussing what the statistical results reveal about the business.

## 6. Expected Learning Output

By the end of Day 1, you should be able to produce:

**1. Business Problem Statement**

A clearly defined business problem translated into an analytical or Data Science objective.

**2. Statistical Summary**

A summary of relevant descriptive statistics with brief interpretations.

**3. Initial Analytical Observations**

Observations that explain what the available data reveals and what additional information may be required.

## 7. Independent Practice / Homework

Identify a business problem from an industry of your choice, such as:

- Retail and E-commerce
- Banking and Financial Services
- Telecommunications
- Manufacturing
- Logistics and Supply Chain
- Customer Service Operations

Prepare a short problem statement covering:

1. What is the business problem?
2. Why is it important to solve?
3. What data might be required?
4. What analytical approach could be considered?
5. How would the organisation measure success?

You are not expected to build a machine learning model for this assignment.

## 8. Knowledge Check

After completing the session, test your understanding:

1. How does Data Science differ from traditional reporting?
2. What are the major stages of the Data Science lifecycle?
3. What is the difference between a population and a sample?
4. When would the median be more informative than the mean?
5. Why does correlation not necessarily imply causation?
